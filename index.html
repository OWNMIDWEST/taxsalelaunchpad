The county database · subscription

Every parcel in the county. Not just the delinquent ones.

We have all parcels in a given county, not just addresses, with displayed data refreshed as often as you like.

  • Linked to the source. Every parcel profile also links to the county tax record, GIS map and property card; changes at the source flow in as often as you need.
  • Common data points on every parcel. Owner of record, mailing address, acreage, assessed value and current tax status — sort by any of them.
  • Multi-year history. Delinquency and auction bid history, so repeat parcels stand out.
  • Tax deed tracking. Which parcels went unredeemed, when the deed issued and to whom — your deeds and everyone else's.
  • Your own tags and statuses. On properties, owners and auction items; sort and search by any of them.
  • Saved searches and bid sheets. Export to Excel or CSV for auction day.
  • Shared team access. A partner or VA works the same list you do.
  • CRM add-on. Pre-sale outreach to delinquent owners with notes, call logs and follow-up tasks.
